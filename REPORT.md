
## Homepage direction update

The homepage now applies the strongest patterns observed in current global technology sites: clear first-screen positioning, a system shown in action, a single primary CTA, progressive disclosure, and motion that guides attention rather than obscuring content. The new scroll progress rail and IntersectionObserver reveals make the page feel directed by the visitor's scroll while keeping copy as real HTML for accessibility and search.

Reference principles reviewed: BlackRock's structured corporate navigation and insight-led content, SpaceX's mission-led hero and product/system storytelling, and Framer's guidance on clear positioning, product-in-context, proof, one primary CTA, purposeful motion, and performance. These references informed structure only; OkzByte's copy, visual identity, and 3D system remain original.
