# OkzByte Terms of Service — Review Report

- The repository contains a React and Vite public website. The new Terms page is available at `/terms-and-conditions`; `/terms` remains an alias. The canonical link and sitemap use the new URL.
- The site markets technology domains that include artificial intelligence, machine learning, blockchain and Web3, fintech, real-world assets, software engineering, cloud infrastructure, cybersecurity, data engineering, digital identity, and research. The page distinguishes these descriptions from a claim that every item is a currently available commercial product.
- No public user-registration, sign-in, or user-account deletion product was found in the reviewed website code. The private admin area is not a public account product.
- No public checkout, subscription, billing, pricing, or payment processor flow was found. The Terms do not invent prices, renewal rules, refund rights, or payment arrangements.
- The public Careers page says there are no verified current openings and offers an expression-of-interest form. It does not advertise a live vacancy or guarantee selection.
- The existing public site uses `hello@okzbyte.com` as its contact address. The reviewed source does not provide a verified registered address, company registration number, or governing jurisdiction.
- The site describes OkzByte as part of a wider ecosystem and references independent external projects. The Terms expressly state that a listing or reference does not automatically establish ownership, control, financial responsibility, technical integration, endorsement, or partnership.
- The public admin dashboard says server-side administrator provisioning and a database connection are required. No policy CMS with draft, preview, publish, archive, or revision controls was verified.
- The Terms page has 24 anchor-linked sections, in-page search, a mobile contents panel, print styling, a browser-print control, and a downloadable PDF. The downloaded PDF is a static version and must be regenerated whenever the published Terms change.
